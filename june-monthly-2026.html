Revenue refund rate followed the same pattern, rising from 3.33% to a peak of 10.07% in Week 3 before easing slightly to 9.41% in Week 4. The monthly blended order refund rate of 9.80% sits within the 5–10% benchmark range, but that average masks a sharp deteriorating trend that needs direct investigation before July.
